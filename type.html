Animals are living things. Animals live all around us. There are many kinds of animals. Some animals are big. Some animals are small. Some animals live on land. Some animals live in water. Some animals live in trees. Animals need food to live. Animals need water to survive. Animals need air to breathe. Animals need a safe place to live. Some animals live in forests. Some animals live in deserts. Some animals live in the sea. Some animals live on farms. Some animals live in our homes. Animals come in many shapes. Animals have different colours. Animals are an important part of nature. Pets A pet is an animal we keep at home. Dogs are popular pets. Dogs are friendly and loyal. Dogs can protect our homes. Dogs like to play with people. Cats are also popular pets. Cats like to sleep a lot. Cats can run and jump. Cats use their whiskers to feel things. Rabbits are small and furry. Rabbits like to eat vegetables. Hamsters are small pets. Birds can make lovely sounds. Fish can live in tanks. Pets need love and care. Pets need clean water. Pets need healthy food. Pets need a clean place to live. We should never hurt animals. Pets can make us happy. Farm Animals Many animals live on farms. Cows are farm animals. Cows give us milk. Calves are baby cows. Sheep have thick wool. Wool can be used to make clothes. Goats can climb rocks. Goats eat plants and leaves. Pigs like to dig in the ground. Horses are strong animals. Horses can run very fast. Donkeys can carry heavy loads. Chickens lay eggs. Ducks have webbed feet. Ducks can swim in water. Geese have long necks. Turkeys are large birds. Farmers take care of farm animals. Farm animals need food and shelter. Farm animals help people in many ways. Wild Animals Wild animals live in nature. Lions are strong animals. Lions are known as the kings of the jungle. Tigers have black stripes. Elephants are very large animals. Elephants have long trunks. Elephants use their trunks to drink water. Giraffes have very long necks. Giraffes eat leaves from tall trees. Monkeys can climb trees. Monkeys enjoy eating fruit. Bears have thick fur. Foxes have bushy tails. Wolves often live in groups. Zebras have black and white stripes. Kangaroos carry babies in their pouches. Deer can run quickly. Hippos spend much time in water. Rhinos have thick skin. Wild animals need freedom and space. Animals in Water Many animals live in water. Fish live in rivers, lakes, and seas. Fish use gills to breathe in water. Sharks live in the sea. Dolphins are clever sea animals. Dolphins breathe air through a blowhole. Whales are very large animals. Whales need air to breathe. Octopuses have eight arms. Crabs have hard shells. Turtles have hard shells. Sea turtles lay eggs on beaches. Frogs can live on land and in water. Frogs catch insects with their tongues. Crocodiles have strong jaws. Jellyfish have soft bodies. Starfish live in the sea. Penguins are birds that cannot fly. Penguins are excellent swimmers. Water animals need clean water. Birds and Insects Birds have feathers. Most birds can fly. Some birds cannot fly. Eagles have strong wings. Owls can see well in low light. Parrots can copy some sounds. Peacocks have beautiful feathers. Sparrows are small birds. Swans often live near lakes. Bees collect nectar from flowers. Bees help flowers grow by pollination. Butterflies have colourful wings. Ants live in groups. Spiders have eight legs. Ladybirds are small insects. Grasshoppers can jump very far. Some insects help plants. Some insects can damage crops. Birds build nests to lay eggs. We should protect birds and insects. Caring for Animals Animals are important to our world. Every animal has a place in nature. We should treat animals kindly. We should not throw rubbish into rivers. Plastic can hurt animals. We should keep forests clean. We should protect animals from danger. We should never destroy animal homes. All animals deserve care and respect. A world with animals is a beautiful world.
